About the game

Gravity is a single-player 3D space game. You start as a tiny rock in the dark, let gravity pull in floating debris, and grow into a planet, a star, a black hole and finally a galaxy. Each level you pick one of three cosmic laws, so every run plays differently. A Daily Universe gives everyone the same universe each day.

How to play

  • Move: press and drag on any empty part of the screen (a floating joystick appears under your thumb).
  • Gravity Collapse: tap the round button at the bottom right when it is charged.
  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons next to the radar.
  • Pause and settings: tap the pause button at the top right. Sound, vibration and reduced flashing can be switched there.
  • Bodies with a red ring are bigger than you: hitting one costs a core. Lose every core and the run ends.

Questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically, on your device only. There is no account and no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices.

How do I reset my progress? Delete the App and install it again. This erases all stardust and unlocked origins and cannot be undone.

When does the Daily Universe change? At midnight Beijing time (UTC+8), so that players everywhere share the same universe on the same day.

The screen flashes too much for me. Pause the game and turn on “Reduce Flashing”.

The game runs slowly or gets warm. Close other apps, and make sure Low Power Mode is off. Older devices automatically use lower graphics settings.

Does the App collect my data? No. See the Privacy Policy.

关于游戏

《引力》是一款单人 3D 太空游戏。开局你只是黑暗里的一颗小石头，让引力把漂浮的碎石拉过来，一路长成行星、恒星、黑洞，最后吞下整个星系。每升一级从三条宇宙法则里挑一条，每局玩法都不一样。“每日宇宙”让所有玩家每天玩到同一个宇宙。

怎么玩

  • 移动：在屏幕空白处按住拖动（拇指下会出现浮动摇杆）。
  • 引力坍缩：充能完成后点右下角的圆形按钮。
  • 缩放：双指捏合，或点雷达旁边的 + / − 按钮。
  • 暂停和设置：点右上角的暂停按钮，在那里可以开关声音、震动和减弱闪光。
  • 带红圈的天体比你大，撞上会碎掉一颗核心，核心碎完这局就结束。

常见问题

进度会保存吗？会，自动保存，只存在你的设备上。没有账号，也没有云存档，所以进度不会在设备之间同步。

怎么重置进度？删除应用后重新安装。这会清掉全部星尘和已解锁的起源，且无法撤销。

每日宇宙什么时候换？北京时间（UTC+8）零点，让各地玩家在同一天玩到同一个宇宙。

画面闪得太厉害。暂停游戏，打开“减弱闪光”。

游戏卡顿或发热。关掉其他应用，并确认没开低电量模式。较旧的设备会自动使用较低的画质。

应用会收集我的数据吗？不会，详见隐私政策。
