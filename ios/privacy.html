Gravity (《引力》) · for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-02 · This version covers the App with optional rewarded video ads.

In short

  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run a game server and we never receive them.
  • There is no account or sign-in. The app does not ask for your name or contacts, and does not access your location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The app shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob: an ad plays only when you tap to watch one (to revive once per run, or to double the stardust from a run). Google collects some device and usage information to deliver and measure these ads (section 3).
  • The app asks for Apple’s permission to track. If you allow it, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads. If you don’t, ads still work but are not personalized with that identifier. In regions where the law requires it, Google’s consent message asks for your choices first.
  • The app has no in-app purchases and no analytics of our own. Its own code sends nothing to us, and we never receive your game data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how “Gravity” (Chinese name 《引力》, the “App”), a casual single-player 3D space game for iPhone distributed through Apple’s App Store, handles information. The App is operated by East Coast Tech Inc., referred to as “we”, “us” or “our”. If you have any questions, contact us at contact@eastbigtech.com.

2. Information saved on your device

To keep your progress, the App saves a small record in its own storage on your device. It contains only game data:

  • your stardust balance;
  • the “origins” (starting bodies) you have unlocked, and the one currently selected;
  • your best run: largest mass, stage reached and time;
  • whether you have finished the tutorial;
  • your Daily Universe record: the most recent day you played it, how many runs you started that day, your best result that day (win or not, time, mass, stage, longest combo, landmarks absorbed), whether that day’s first-run bonus was collected, your streak of consecutive days, and the last day you finished a Daily Universe run.

Your settings (sound on or off, vibration, reduced flashing) are saved in a second small record on your device.

These records contain no name, contact details, account identifiers or device identifiers. The App does not upload them, and we never receive them.

Daily features. To decide what “today” is, the App uses your device’s clock, converted to Beijing time (UTC+8), so everyone gets the same Daily Universe on the same day. The Daily Universe is generated from the date, with no server involved.

Technical information. The App and its game engine read basic technical information, such as screen size, safe-area insets, system language and graphics capabilities, to display the App in your language and choose quality settings. The App does not store this information or send it to us.

Device backups. If you back up your device with iCloud or to a computer, your backup may include the App’s saved records, under Apple’s and your own backup settings. We do not have access to your backups.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform SDK, provided by Google. We use them only for rewarded video ads. You decide whether to watch one: when your last core shatters, the App offers to revive you once per run if you watch an ad, and the results screen offers to double the stardust from that run. You can always say no and keep playing. The App does not show banner or interstitial ads.

To load, show, measure and limit these ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK sends information from your device to Google. According to Google’s disclosures, this can include:

  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general (coarse) location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, app name and version;
  • identifiers: Apple’s identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were requested and shown, video views, taps and when they happened;
  • diagnostic information about the SDK, such as crash and performance data.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, cap how often you see the same ad, detect fraud and abuse, and improve its services. Ads may come from Google or from other advertisers and ad networks through Google’s auction; ad networks may use Apple’s SKAdNetwork, which measures ad results without identifying you. Google acts as an independent third party and processes this information under its own privacy policy; see also Google’s page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Tracking permission. The first time you open the App, iOS asks whether to allow the App to track you. If you allow it, Google may use your advertising identifier to show you ads based on your activity in other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the App cannot access your advertising identifier and ads are not personalized with it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

We share this information only with Google, and only for the advertising purposes described here. We work only with ad partners that protect your data to the same or an equal standard as this policy: Google is bound by its own privacy policy and by the Google Ads data protection terms that apply to AdMob.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. In these regions (and anywhere else Google requires it) the App shows Google’s consent message before ads use your personal data. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the start screen also shows an “Ad Privacy Settings” button so you can review or change your choices at any time.

How to limit personalized ads. You can:

  • choose “Ask App Not to Track” when iOS asks, or later turn off tracking for the App in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking;
  • where it is shown, use the “Ad Privacy Settings” button on the start screen to review or change your choices in Google’s consent message;
  • manage how Google personalizes ads for your Google Account at My Ad Center.

Ad content. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for general audiences (Google’s “G” ad content rating).

What we receive. We only see aggregated AdMob reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned. They do not identify you, and we cannot link them to your game data.

If you prefer not to share this information, simply don’t watch ads. The App then loads ads in the background but you never have to watch one; to stop ad requests entirely you can turn on Airplane Mode while playing (the game works offline).

4. What we do not collect

Apart from the advertising information that Google’s SDK handles as described in section 3, we do not collect, receive or store through the App:

  • your name, email address, phone number, Apple ID or contacts;
  • your precise location, camera, microphone, photos or other files;
  • payment information (there are no in-app purchases);
  • chat messages or user-generated content (there are no such features);
  • analytics of our own: the App contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK other than the advertising SDK above.

Besides the ad SDK’s requests, the App only goes online when you tap “Privacy Policy” on the start screen, which opens this page in your web browser.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message; see sections 6 and 8.

5. Apple

Apple distributes the App and processes information about your purchase, download and use of the App Store under its own terms and privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App through App Store Connect. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the App. You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

6. How long data is kept

The saved records stay on your device until you delete the App. We keep no copy. Google keeps advertising information according to its own retention policies. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up.

7. How to delete your data

Your game data exists only on your device, so you control it directly: deleting the App from your device deletes its saved records (a device backup you made may still contain a copy). Deleting resets all progress, including stardust and unlocked origins, and cannot be undone; we cannot restore it because we have no copy. Questions: contact@eastbigtech.com.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not ask anyone for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and international users

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

We hold no personal information about you from the App, so there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete: your progress is on your device and you can delete it yourself (see section 7). For advertising information, Google is the party that holds it; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads use your personal data only on the basis of the choices you make in Google’s consent message, which you can change with “Ad Privacy Settings”. You can make requests about that information to Google through its privacy policy, and you can always limit it with the tracking setting described in section 3. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, based on our legitimate interest in responding (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your emails are stored with our email service provider, which processes them on our behalf and may store them outside your country; you can ask us to delete them. Saving progress on your device is strictly necessary for the game you chose to play. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information for money. US state privacy laws: if you allow tracking, Google’s use of your advertising identifier and ad interaction data for personalized ads may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under laws such as California’s. You can opt out at any time by choosing “Ask App Not to Track” or turning off tracking for the App in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; where Google shows a US state privacy message, you can also use “Ad Privacy Settings” on the start screen.

10. Security

Your saved records are protected by your device’s security, including iOS app sandboxing. The App does not encrypt them, but they contain no information that identifies you. Because the App’s own code sends no data to us, there is no copy on our side that could be exposed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add features. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. Before adding any feature that sends information to us or collects new kinds of information, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent in the App.

contact@eastbigtech.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-02 · 本版本适用于带可选激励视频广告的应用。

简要说明

  • 你的游戏进度和设置只保存在你自己的设备上。我们没有游戏服务器，也从不会收到这些数据。
  • 没有账号，不需要登录。本应用不会要你的姓名或联系人，也不会访问你的位置、照片、相机或麦克风。
  • 本应用有来自 Google AdMob 的可选激励视频广告：只有你点了要看，广告才会播放（每局一次看广告复活，或在结算页把这局的星尘翻倍）。为了投放和衡量这些广告，Google 会收集一些设备和使用信息（见第 3 条）。
  • 本应用会通过 Apple 的系统弹窗询问能否追踪。你允许的话，Google 可能用设备的广告标识符给你看更相关的广告；不允许的话，广告照常可以看，只是不会用这个标识符做个性化。在法律要求的地区，Google 的同意弹窗会先征求你的选择。
  • 没有应用内购买，也没有我们自己的统计分析。本应用自己的代码不向我们发送任何东西，我们从不会收到你的游戏数据。

1. 我们是谁

本隐私政策说明《引力》（英文名 Gravity，下称“本应用”）如何处理信息。本应用是一款通过 Apple App Store 发行、适用于 iPhone 的休闲单人 3D 太空游戏，由 East Coast Tech Inc.（下称“我们”）运营。如有任何问题，请联系 contact@eastbigtech.com。

2. 保存在你设备上的信息

为了保留进度，本应用会在它自己在你设备上的存储空间里保存一条很小的记录，里面只有游戏数据：

  • 星尘余额；
  • 已解锁的“起源”（开局天体）以及当前选中的那一个；
  • 最佳一局：最大质量、到达的阶段和用时；
  • 是否已完成新手引导；
  • 每日宇宙记录：最近一次玩每日宇宙那天的日期编号、那天开了几局、那天的最佳成绩（是否通关、用时、质量、阶段、最长连击、吞掉的地标数）、那天的首局奖励是否已领、连续天数，以及最后一次打完每日宇宙的日期。

你的设置（声音开关、震动、减弱闪光）另存为设备上的第二条小记录。

这些记录不包含姓名、联系方式、账号标识或设备标识。本应用不会上传它们，我们也从不会收到它们。

每日功能。为了确定“今天”是哪一天，本应用使用你设备的时钟并换算成北京时间（UTC+8），让所有玩家在同一天拿到同一个每日宇宙。每日宇宙由日期生成，不经过任何服务器。

技术信息。本应用及其游戏引擎会读取设备的基本技术信息（如屏幕尺寸、安全区域、系统语言、图形能力），用来以你的语言显示画面和选择画质。本应用不会保存这些信息，也不会把它们发送给我们。

设备备份。如果你用 iCloud 或电脑备份设备，备份里可能包含本应用保存的记录，这由 Apple 和你自己的备份设置决定。我们无法访问你的备份。

3. 广告（Google AdMob）

本应用内置了 Google 提供的 Google 移动广告 SDK（AdMob）和用户消息平台 SDK（User Messaging Platform），只用来播放激励视频广告。看不看由你决定：最后一颗核心碎掉时，本应用会问你要不要看一段广告复活（每局一次）；结算页会问你要不要看一段广告把这局的星尘翻倍。你随时可以拒绝，继续玩。本应用没有横幅广告和插屏广告。

为了加载、播放、衡量和限制这些广告并防止作弊，该 SDK 会把你设备上的一些信息发送给 Google。根据 Google 的说明，可能包括：

  • 你的 IP 地址，Google 可能据此估计你的大致（粗略）位置；
  • 设备和应用信息，例如设备型号、操作系统及版本、语言、屏幕尺寸、应用名称和版本；
  • 标识符：Apple 的供应商标识符（IDFV），以及仅在你允许追踪时的设备广告标识符（IDFA）；
  • 广告互动数据，例如请求和展示了哪些广告、视频观看、点击及其时间；
  • SDK 的诊断信息，例如崩溃和性能数据。

Google 可能用这些信息来投放和衡量广告、控制同一广告出现的频率、发现作弊和滥用，以及改进其服务。广告可能来自 Google，也可能来自通过 Google 竞价投放的其他广告主和广告网络；广告网络可能使用 Apple 的 SKAdNetwork，它在不识别你的情况下衡量广告效果。Google 作为独立的第三方，按其自己的隐私权政策处理这些信息，另见 Google 的说明页《Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息》。

追踪授权。你第一次打开本应用时，iOS 会询问是否允许本应用追踪你。你允许的话，Google 可能用你的广告标识符，根据你在其他公司的应用和网站上的活动给你看广告；选择“要求 App 不跟踪”的话，本应用无法获得你的广告标识符，广告也不会用它做个性化。你可以随时在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 跟踪”里更改。

我们只和 Google 共享这些信息，而且只用于这里写的广告用途。我们只与能以本政策相同或同等标准保护你数据的广告合作方合作：Google 受其自己的隐私权政策以及适用于 AdMob 的 Google 广告数据保护条款约束。

欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士的同意。在这些地区（以及 Google 要求的其他地区），本应用会在广告使用你的个人数据之前显示 Google 的同意弹窗。在需要的地区（例如欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士），开始界面上还会显示“广告隐私设置”按钮，你可以随时查看或更改选择。

如何限制个性化广告。你可以：

  • 在 iOS 询问时选择“要求 App 不跟踪”，或之后在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 跟踪”里关掉本应用的跟踪；
  • 如果开始界面上有“广告隐私设置”按钮，用它查看或更改你在 Google 同意弹窗里的选择；
  • 在 Google 我的广告中心管理 Google 如何为你的 Google 账号个性化广告。

广告内容。本应用里的广告仅限适合一般大众的内容（Google 广告内容分级的“G”级）。

我们能看到什么。我们只能看到 AdMob 的汇总报表，例如展示了多少次广告、收入多少。这些报表不会识别出你，我们也无法把它们和你的游戏数据对应起来。

如果你不想分享这些信息，不看广告就行：本应用会在后台加载广告，但你永远不必观看；想完全不发广告请求，可以在玩的时候打开飞行模式（游戏可以离线玩）。
